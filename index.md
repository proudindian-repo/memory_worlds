1. Information We Collect
No Account Required: We do not require you to create an account, register, or sign in to use the App.
No Direct Personal Data Collection: We do not directly request, collect, or store personal information such as your real name, phone number, postal address, email, or passwords.
Local On-Device Storage: All gameplay progress, mastered worlds, level star ratings, streaks, cognitive metrics, XP, and custom user preferences are stored exclusively on your device in a sandboxed, private SQLite database.
Third-Party Advertising: The App uses third-party advertising services provided by Google (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob) to display advertisements and optional rewarded bonus hints. These services may process certain technical device information automatically when you use the App.
Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. Depending on the device, SDK configuration, user age settings, and location, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as:

IP address (used to infer coarse location and prevent fraudulent traffic).
Device and account identifiers, including Android Advertising ID (AAID) for non-child audiences.
App and device telemetry (e.g., operating system version, device model, screen resolution).
Information about ad interactions (impressions, clicks, and completed rewarded video views).
Diagnostic, crash, and performance telemetry related to ad delivery.
Fraud detection and security signals to prevent bot traffic and invalid ad clicks.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertising and to protect against abuse.

For details on data processed by Google Mobile Ads, visit: Google AdMob Play Data Disclosure.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Google Mobile Ads is used strictly for:

Displaying contextual and non-disruptive advertisements.
Measuring ad delivery performance and verification.
Providing personalized or non-personalized advertising where permitted by law and age settings.
Granting in-game rewarded hints when a rewarded ad is completed.
Preventing invalid click fraud, bot traffic, and abuse.
Maintaining advertising security and SDK performance.
3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties or commercial data brokers.

Third-party processing occurs through Google Mobile Ads under Google's privacy commitments.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising and User Choices

Depending on applicable laws, user age classifications, and device settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Users can manage or reset their advertising identifier and opt out of personalized ads through their device settings, where available:

Android Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

The exact settings and options may vary by Android version and device.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Memory Worlds is designed as a family-friendly cognitive puzzle game suitable for audiences of all ages.

We employ a neutral age selection setting within the App. For players classified as under 13:

All ad requests are flagged with TagForChildDirectedTreatment.yes in compliance with COPPA.
The maximum ad content rating is strictly enforced to "G" (General Audiences).
Personalized advertising and interest-based tracking are disabled.
Advertising identifiers (AAID) are zeroed out and not transmitted by the App's child-directed advertising configuration.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently submitted, please contact us at proudindian.tech.person@gmail.com, and we will take appropriate steps to address the concern.

6. Data Retention, Local Control & Deletion

Because Memory Worlds does not maintain an online account system or remote database servers, we do not retain gameplay records on our own servers.

You maintain direct control over your on-device data:

Data Export: You can export your gameplay history and session records in JSON or CSV format directly from the in-game Personal Dashboard.
Instant Data Erasure: You can permanently erase local save progress, stars, settings, and high scores by selecting the confirmed "Erase All Data" option in the Dashboard.
App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application generally removes its local SQLite databases and app-specific cached assets from your device. Backups or exported files stored elsewhere may remain.
7. Data Security

We store gameplay progress locally within Android's sandboxed application storage. Network interactions initiated by Google Mobile Ads use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit where supported by the service.

Although we take reasonable measures to protect information, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Medical & Health Disclaimer

Memory Worlds is strictly an entertainment and cognitive recreation puzzle game. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, or clinical therapy system. The App is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

9. Internet Connection

Memory Worlds is designed to be playable offline. An active Internet connection is required for online advertising features, including Google Mobile Ads banners, interstitials, and rewarded hint videos.

Core gameplay does not require an Internet connection, although advertising features will not be available when offline.
